# Income claims from interviews, podcasts, newsletters, long-form press — TikTok Shop affiliate (AI/faceless + human benchmarks)

Access date for all sources: 2026-09-24. Method: WebSearch budget was exhausted (200/200) before this sub-task started, so search was done via DuckDuckGo-HTML (curl), NexLev youtube_search (EN + DE), NexLev bulk transcripts (24 videos condensed to /scratchpad/yt_tx2/<id>.txt), NexLev youtube_video_details for publish dates, WebFetch for articles, and tt_profile.sh / tt_video.sh for TikTok verification.
Tag legend: **Claimed** = asserted by claimant; **Verified** = seen in primary/TikTok data; **Estimated** = my calculation (formula shown). Paywalled originals (WSJ, Business Insider, Independent) could not be fetched; the WSJ text was read from the Charm.io reprint (blog.charm.io), which quotes the WSJ article at length.

Terminology: TikTok Shop Affiliate Center shows **GMV** (order value attributed to the creator) and **Est. commission** (creator's cut, typically 5–30 %). Podcast hosts frequently say "made $X" while the number is GMV. Where the speaker makes the distinction, I record it; otherwise "ambiguous".

Deduplication note: tommycetty (kjCyL4Vn4P4, 1XO4S9ZKLDY), THE ECOM KING (W5ThaDXg2ss) and four other YouTube challenge videos are already covered in research/claims_youtube.md and are NOT repeated here.
