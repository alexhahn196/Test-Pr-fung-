# Claims from analytics platforms, official TikTok data and documented press (AI-content TikTok Shop affiliates + human benchmarks)

Access date for all sources: 2026-09-24. Method: WebSearch budget was exhausted, so discovery ran via Google News RSS / Bing News RSS (`scratchpad/newsrss.py`) and direct curl/WebFetch of the articles (`scratchpad/fetchtxt.py`; raw HTML+text saved under `research/press/<hash>.html|.txt`). Tag legend: **Verified** = seen in primary data (TikTok profile API, official newsroom, primary document); **Claimed** = asserted by the claimant / reported by the outlet without independent proof; **Estimated** = my own calculation, formula shown.

Terminology: TikTok Shop "sales", "revenue" and "GMV" in analytics tools and TikTok statements all mean gross merchandise value attributed to the creator's content. Creator commission is typically 5–30 % of that (eMarketer/Momentum Works: average 13.1 % in 2025). "Net profit"/"commission" claims are marked separately.

Not accessible from this environment: kalodata.com (Cloudflare 403), thelowdown.momentum.asia (403), tubefilter.com (403), wsj.com (paywall 401), nytimes.com (403), inc.com (403), chosun.com (404 – content recovered via MSN syndication API). The EchoTik "TikTok Shop 2025 Q1 Report" PDF already in the scratchpad (`echotik_q1_2025.pdf`, extracted to `echotik_q1_2025.txt`) covers Southeast Asia only (creator counts per country, e.g. Thailand 850,000 creators by March 2025) and contains no US creator-earnings data. No Kalodata/FastMoss/EchoTik/Tabcut report on creator GMV *distribution* (share of affiliates > $1k/month) was found publicly; the only distribution-type data points come from TikTok itself (16,000 creators > six figures GMV), the Swoveralls/DudeRobe field report, DemandSage (weakly sourced) and the Biverse/Chosun AI-video analysis.

## C. Synthesis for the due-diligence question (AI/faceless affiliate income)
1. No analytics platform (Kalodata/FastMoss/EchoTik/Tabcut) publishes a creator-level distribution of affiliate earnings; the closest public numbers are: TikTok's "16,000 creators > $100k GMV" (≈1.7 % of ~945k US affiliates with sales, Estimated), Swoveralls' "99 % of enrolled affiliates generated no sales", and Biverse's AI-video tally (0.15 % of selling AI videos > $10k GMV in Aug 2026).
2. The only documented AI-specific earnings evidence from a journalist is BI's "tens of thousands of dollars in product sales" (GMV, screenshot viewed, anonymous) and Chosun/Biverse's single $89,000-in-30-days video (GMV). All larger AI figures ($51k/month profit, $67,420 per video, "$400k paid out last month") come from course sellers / the brand recruiting affiliates and were withdrawn (videos deleted) after press inquiries.
3. Human top-creator benchmarks show what volume is needed: top-10 monthly independent creators run $1M–$4M GMV/month on 30M–200M content views/month (≈ $13–$54 GMV per 1k views for mainstream products), with thousands of lifetime videos; a $100k/month commission (Brandon Hans) implies ≈ $0.7–1M GMV/month.
4. Structural constraints for AI/faceless: LIVE AI voices banned (May 2026), shoppable-video quality rules (face + physical product + dynamic footage), brand-level AI bans with commission clawback (SharkNinja), PPS/AHR scoring, and 60 % of a new user's FYP already being AI content (Kapwing) → saturation.
