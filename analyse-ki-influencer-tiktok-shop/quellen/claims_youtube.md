# YouTube income claims: AI / faceless TikTok Shop affiliate (and human benchmarks)

Access date for all sources: 2026-09-24. Method: NexLev youtube_search (13 queries, EN + DE, uploads <= 1 year), transcripts via get_bulk_video_transcripts (40 videos), grep + full read of the challenge-style videos. Tag legend: **Claimed** = asserted by claimant in video; **Verified** = seen in primary data (none here — all YouTube claims are self-reported); **Estimated** = my own calculation with formula shown.
Publish dates are approximated from YouTube's relative "x months ago" at access date (2026-09-24).

Important terminology note: TikTok Shop Affiliate Center shows **GMV** (gross merchandise value of orders attributed to the creator) and **Est. commission** (creator's cut, typically 5–30 %). Many YouTubers say "I made $X" while showing GMV. Where the transcript says which, I note it; where a screenshot is described but not verifiable from the transcript, I say "screenshot claimed (not verifiable from transcript)".

## C. Cross-cutting observations
1. Nearly every large claim (>$10k/month) comes from someone selling a course/mentorship/tool; the independent or tool-employee tests (Turner $339/7d, ECOM KING $200–300/30d, Patryk $2,700 GMV/$199 commission in Dec 2025 then $13k GMV/$2k commission in Apr 2026, Deniz £25/5d, Nadorb £2/7d, Mikey $2.2k/week with one viral video) cluster at $0–3k/month commission.
2. GMV vs commission: titles almost always use GMV ("$33.9k in 7 days", "$60K in 10 days", "$450,063"); commission is 10–30% of that (Patryk 15%, tommycetty ~20%, tkay 30%, Mikey ~15%, Adkins 16%, Obiefuna 15%). Bel's rule of thumb: GMV 100k -> take-home 10k.
3. Volume drivers that recur: 5–10 AI videos/day/account, several accounts (3–15), bought accounts ($200–400), reliance on brand GMV Max ad spend behind converting videos, 90/10 rule (few videos make most commissions), frequent bans/violations (tommycetty main account banned, Cal Kramer compliance ban, Ollie 6 accounts banned, Harun link removed).
4. Per-1k-view economics quoted: tommycetty $7.41 net/1k views (best) and "$5–10 per 10k views" typical; Cal Kramer 1 sale/2k views on $1,000 couch; Deniz 1 sale/1k views on £3 cards; Josh Adkins ~£1,600 commission from ~300k views (≈£5/1k views).
5. Evidence quality: transcripts describe dashboards (Affiliate Center "Est. commission"/GMV, Kalodata) but nothing is independently verifiable; no bank statements or payout screenshots described in any transcript. All amounts = Claimed.
