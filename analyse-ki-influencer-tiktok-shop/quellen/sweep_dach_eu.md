# Sweep DACH / EU / UK: AI-/faceless TikTok-Shop-Accounts (Modality: German-language + UK/FR/ES/IT)

Access date for all sources: 2026-09-24. Tag legend: **Verified** = seen in TikTok public data (tt_profile.sh / tt_video.sh) or a primary document; **Claimed** = asserted by the person/creator/vendor; **Estimated** = own calculation (formula shown).

## 0. Method and tool constraints (important for reading this file)
- WebSearch budget was already exhausted (200/200) when this sweep started. Bing (direct + RSS) returns junk for German quoted queries, DuckDuckGo/Startpage/Google/Yandex/Mojeek/Ecosia block bots, Reddit blocks fetches, Brave Search works via WebFetch but rate-limits after ~1 request (HTTP 429) - only 3 Brave result pages were obtained.
- NexLev `watch_youtube_video_and_ask` daily limit (15/15) was already used up by other agents -> on-screen TikTok handles in YouTube videos could NOT be read. Transcripts (40 videos, DE/EN/FR/ES/IT) were pulled with `get_bulk_video_transcripts` and grepped; spoken handles are rare.
- Handles below were sanity-checked with `tt_profile.sh` (statusCode 0 = exists; 10221 = not found; 10222 = exists but restricted/"ErrBizUserSecret").
- Consequence: this sweep yields (a) verified virtual-influencer accounts (none sell via TikTok Shop), (b) verified DACH/EU creator/coach accounts that openly build AI TikTok-Shop content, (c) named-but-unverifiable example accounts from webinars, and (d) many German income claims with test-account results (incl. failures). It does NOT yield a list of anonymous German AI-avatar affiliate accounts with handles - those are shown only on screen in YouTube videos and in Kalodata (paywalled).

## 7. Cross-cutting observations for the report
1. In DACH the visible AI-TikTok-Shop scene is coach-driven (tkay, Khalilo, Nils Becker/Creatorflow, Rohel, TikFluencer, Deniz Sancar, Taro). Their own public TikTok accounts are either human creator accounts or tiny; the AI affiliate accounts are kept anonymous and shown only on screen.
2. Claimed German AI results are small (179 € / 7 days; 3-400 €/day for a 2-3-account operation; 600 € from one 2 M-view AI video) versus the big USD figures that German coaches (tkay, Osher) achieve in the **US** shop with bought/US accounts.
3. TikTok's own free Symphony Creative Studio avatar feature is the recommended entry tool in DE (Nils Becker); Higgsfield, Kalodata, Claude/ChatGPT, Google Flow/Veo, ElevenLabs, HeyGen, Arcads, MaxFusion, Oumomo are the others.
4. Failure patterns documented: views without income (MarcIt), Instagram KI-Influencerin with 30 followers after 4 weeks (ARD), account bans ("Account auf einmal gesperrt", Nils Becker interview), 1,500-follower gate in DE, US-account purchases.
